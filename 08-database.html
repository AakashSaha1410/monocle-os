A tableis like one tab in a spreadsheet — one kind of thing: loans, people, emails.
A rowis one of those things — this loan, this guarantor, this email.
A columnis one fact about it — the address, the date it was sent.
A linkis a column that points at a row in another table — "this email belongs to that loan".
