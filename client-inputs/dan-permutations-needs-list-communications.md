# Dan Frankel — permutations, standard needs list, communications, attorney states

**Filed:** 2026-09-15, from Aakash. Dan's message to Jonathan, plus the reply
listing the attorney states. The send date was not recorded; the filing date is
used for authority ordering.

**What it is:** Dan's own written answer to three things the build had been
waiting on — the permutation matrix (Q-001), his document catalogue (Q-021), and
the full list of communications with sender and recipient for each. It also
settles which states get a Legal Kick-off (Q-009).

**Authority:** this is the newest source. Where it differs from anything earlier
it wins. Rendered end to end in `docs/10-FLOWCHART.md`.

**What it changes or adds against the earlier sources:**

- The needs list is now defined by Dan directly — a standard list plus
  permutation add-ons — rather than assembled from what the calls named.
- The permutation branches are simpler than the nine add-on flags of 31 July:
  purchase/refinance · mid-construction refinance · direct/broker · guarantors
  more than one · adding square footage · light/heavy reno or GUC · DSCR.
- Track record splits by product: DSCR needs 24 months of investment experience
  including leases; RTL needs stabilized or sold properties in the past 36 months.
- The broker permutation captures eight fields.
- Communications not previously named: Loan Pre-Approved (Credit → LO), Hand-off
  (Credit → CM), Internal and External Loan Structure Approval Requests, Setpoint
  Request, Internal Final Approval, Final Approval to Investor with a
  configurable sender, Approved Terms, Loan Docs to Escrow, Request LOE,
  Order updated estimated closing statement.
- Senders that moved: the Construction (budget) Order and the Pre-Processing
  email are from Credit; the recurring needs list is on behalf of Credit.
- The recurring needs list cadence: daily at a set time, every workday, or
  specific days and times each week.
