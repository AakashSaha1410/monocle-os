> "Really have this be focused on the **pipeline, email, email templates,
> document management, LOI generation, loan doc generation, and these like six
> follow-up emails**."

generation · pipeline management · embedded email with templates and an
auto-send needs list · document management · loan doc generation · some reporting.

## The three tests

Anything proposed for Phase 1 must pass one:

1. Does it make the process visible?
2. Does it get documents in faster, or with less client friction?
3. Does it let the pipeline be managed in one place?
